# Contributing

Thanks for considering a contribution to FrySafe Kerala.

## Useful contribution areas

- KiCad schematic, PCB layout, DRC, and fabrication notes.
- FDC1004 driver and ESP32-S3 firmware.
- Electrode geometry and measurement repeatability.
- Sample chamber and temperature measurement.
- Data schemas, analysis scripts, plots, and documentation.

## Before opening an issue

Include board and firmware revision, sensor/electrode revision, wiring and supply voltage, neutral sample ID, temperature, environmental conditions, raw readings, and exact reproduction steps. Do not include personal/vendor allegations.

## Pull requests

- Keep changes focused and explain the engineering reason.
- Include build/test evidence where practical.
- Preserve raw data and metadata.
- Do not present prototype readings as official TPC or food-safety results.

## Scientific integrity

Distinguish measured facts, hypotheses, estimates, and unknowns. Report negative results. Do not claim safety, regulatory compliance, medical outcomes, or laboratory equivalence without appropriate validation.
