# Project scope and research questions

## Objective

Investigate whether a custom interdigitated electrode measured by the FDC1004 can produce stable, repeatable capacitance readings across edible-oil samples and controlled temperatures.

## In scope for V1

- Electronics bring-up and I2C verification.
- Electrode geometry comparison.
- Repeatability, drift, and temperature characterization.
- Raw data logging with metadata.
- Reproducible hardware and firmware documentation.

## Out of scope for V1

- Consumer food-safety verdict.
- Automatic TPC percentage estimation.
- FSSAI compliance or certification.
- Medical, cancer, or kidney-health inference.
- Public ranking or accusation of restaurants/vendors.

## Research questions

1. Are readings stable over time with a fixed electrode and sample?
2. How sensitive are readings to temperature?
3. Do IDE geometry variants produce distinguishable and repeatable responses?
4. How much variation is caused by sample placement, moisture, residue, and handling?
5. Can capacitance correlate with an accepted reference measurement across independent samples?

A negative or weak correlation is a valid result and should be documented.
