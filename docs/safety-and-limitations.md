# Safety, regulatory and claims limitations

FrySafe Kerala V1 is a research prototype. It is not a certified instrument, diagnostic device, or food-safety decision system.

- Do not use readings to declare food or an establishment safe or unsafe.
- Do not claim FSSAI approval or regulatory compliance.
- Do not infer TPC percentage from capacitance without a validated calibration model.
- Do not claim detection of cancer risk, kidney disease, toxins, or other health outcomes.
- Do not publish allegations about a named vendor based on prototype readings.
- Do not return test samples to consumption.
- The custom PCB electrode is not certified as food-contact material.
- Solvent extraction requires a suitable laboratory, trained supervision, and risk controls. It is not a home experiment.

FSSAI's used-cooking-oil TPC guidance is relevant context for properly measured frying oil; it does not validate this prototype or make its readings regulatory evidence.
