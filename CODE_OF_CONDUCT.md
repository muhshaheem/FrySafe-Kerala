# Code of Conduct

We aim to maintain a respectful, evidence-driven environment.

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique technical ideas, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and reproducible reasoning.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish identifying allegations about businesses or individuals based on prototype measurements.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks, and knowingly publishing fabricated or misleading results are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this policy and may restrict participation where necessary.
